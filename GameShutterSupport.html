Tap the highlight button (the orange button with a running figure) at any moment during recording. GameShutter will automatically export a 10-second clip around that moment when you stop recording.
Highlight clips are saved to your Photos library inside a folder called GameShutter, organized into albums by date (e.g. 2026_11_01). You can also browse them directly inside the GameShutter app using the video editor.
Yes. Tap the Pause button during recording to pause. Tap the Record button again to resume. The pause gap is removed from the final video automatically — it plays back seamlessly.
Highlights marked in the first 9 seconds of a recording are skipped, since there isn't enough footage before the marked moment to fill the clip. Try marking highlights after at least 10 seconds of recording.
Tap the film strip icon next to the settings gear on the camera screen. This opens your highlight library. Tap a date folder to open its clips in the editor, or tap the + card to start a blank project. In the editor you can trim clips, add background music, and export at 720p, 1080p, or 4K.
After exporting from the editor, a share card appears with an Instagram button and a More button. Instagram opens the app with your video pre-selected. More opens the iOS share sheet, which includes TikTok, WhatsApp, AirDrop, Messages, and other apps you have installed.
