Privacy Policy

Transparency. Privacy. Your Game.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

GameShutter is a video recording app designed for sports. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is used.

01. Overview

GameShutter is built with privacy in mind. We believe your recordings and personal information should remain yours. This policy explains how the app operates and how your information is handled.

02. Data Collection

GameShutter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

03. Camera and Microphone

GameShutter requires access to your device's camera and microphone to record video. Recordings are saved directly to your Photos library and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

04. Photos Library

GameShutter requires access to your Photos library to save recorded videos and highlight clips, and to read clips when using the video editor. The app does not read, access, or transmit any photos or videos beyond what you explicitly choose to work with inside the app.

05. No Accounts Required

GameShutter does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

06. No Third-Party Services

GameShutter does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party.

07. No Network Requests

GameShutter does not make any network requests. It works entirely offline.

08. Children's Privacy

GameShutter does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

09. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
